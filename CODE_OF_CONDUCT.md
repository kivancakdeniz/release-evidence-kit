# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Project discussions must be technical, specific, and respectful. Participants should:

- critique claims, designs, and evidence rather than people,
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest,
- distinguish verified facts from assumptions and preferences,
- preserve private or sensitive evaluation data,
- make room for objections and document unresolved disagreement,
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project events or communication channels operated by the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Conduct reports should use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel when public disclosure would expose a target or sensitive information. Publicly observable moderation concerns may be raised in an issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, pause a discussion, reject a contribution, or restrict participation when needed to protect participants and the project. Enforcement decisions may be appealed using the process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

A dedicated Community Specification working group will adopt its required conduct contacts and notices before normative specification contributions open.
